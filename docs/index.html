Questions we get asked most

Coming alone? Most people do.

Still unsure? Read how it all works or message us on WhatsApp — a real person answers.

Will it be safe?
Every plan happens at a public, checked venue with a verified host there from start to finish. You can report anything, any time — we act the same day.
Will there be other women?
Yes. We balance every group and many plans have more women than men. There are also women-only plans every week.
Can I bring a friend?
Of course — tick "bringing a friend" when you request. We just ask that you both mingle with the table.
Who sees my number?
Only your host, and only for that plan. It's never shown to other guests or shared with anyone.
What if I'm an introvert?
Then you're our favourite kind of guest. Plans like the Introverts' Dinner are designed so you can listen first and talk when you feel like it.

Before you go.

📝How seat requests work
Request a seat, the host checks the group mix and confirms on WhatsApp. You pay only once you're in.
✔️Verified hosts
Every host has had a call with our team and runs a trial plan before going public.
🏠A host at every plan
No plan runs without its host present, start to finish.
📍Where you'll meet
Cafés, courts, studios and rooftops we've checked ourselves. The exact address is shared after confirmation.
👥Group size
Mostly 8–16 people — small enough that you actually talk to everyone.
📜House rules, enforced
Be kind, be on time, no pushy dating or selling. Breaking them means you won't be invited back.
⭐Show-up karma
Guests who show up get priority for popular plans. No-shows go to the back of the waitlist.
🚩Report anything
Message us on WhatsApp during or after any plan. We take every report seriously.
💸What you'll pay
Usually a ₹99 seat reservation. Food and drinks are ordered separately unless the plan says "per person".
