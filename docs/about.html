How it works

Making friends as an adult is weirdly hard.

So we made it easy: small groups, a plan worth showing up for, and a host who makes sure nobody's left standing in a corner.

01

Browse

Something on every day — dinners, games, sports, walks, workshops and weekend trips.

02

Request

Send a seat request. The host checks the group mix and confirms on WhatsApp. Then you pay.

03

Show up

Your host does the introductions. You just have to say yes to the second plan.

Help & safety

Safe by design.

Verified hosts
Every host has a call with our team and a trial plan before they go public. Hosts with poor feedback are removed.
Public, checked venues
Plans happen at places we've visited ourselves. Trips are run by our own team with vetted stays.
Balanced groups & women-only plans
Hosts balance each table, and there are women-only plans every week in every city.
Your number stays private
Only your host sees it, only for that plan. No guest lists are shared.
Report anything
Message us on WhatsApp any time — during or after a plan. We respond the same day and can block people from future plans.
The small print

Terms & privacy.

Payments & refunds
Seat reservations are paid after your host confirms and are non-refundable once paid. If we cancel a plan, you get a full refund.
What we store
This website saves your saved plans and requests only in your own browser. Requests are sent through WhatsApp; we keep your name and number only to run the plans you join.
Age
All plans are for adults 18 and over.
